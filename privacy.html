Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

English

Bird Writer does not collect any personal information or usage data.

  • All of your data — text, images, and settings — is stored only on your Mac.
  • The app makes no network connections of any kind (no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no data transmission).
  • No account is required.
  • Exported files (.txt / .bwt) are saved only to locations you choose and are never accessible to the developer.

Contact: ryota.yokoiwa@gmail.com
